## Scope Notes

The manifest is restricted to decision-tree algorithms and tree-specific ensemble algorithms. It includes classical tree induction, split criteria, pruning, oblique and model trees, scalable tree construction, random forests, boosted tree induction, optimal sparse trees, specialized forests, and streaming trees.

Excluded material includes software-package comparisons, non-tree predictors, and hybrid methods whose learned model is not a decision tree or an ensemble of decision trees.
